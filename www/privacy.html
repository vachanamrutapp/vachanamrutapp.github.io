Privacy Policy

Effective October 18, 2026

This policy applies to the Vachanamrut app and the website vachanamrut.in.

In short: we don't collect any personal information. There are no accounts, no sign-in and no ads, and nothing is sold or shared.

Your settings stay with you

Your favourites, bookmarks, reading progress and settings are saved only on your own device. They are never sent to us. To clear them, use Settings → Reset App Data or uninstall the app.

Visit count

We count how many times the app and website are opened. This count is anonymous: it doesn't include your name or anything else that identifies you.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
